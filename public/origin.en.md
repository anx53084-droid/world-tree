**Founding Statement of Guangqi**

Guangqi is not the name of a tree, but the establishing of a relationship: light is willing to linger somewhere, and takes that lingering itself as its way of being.

### Vision
Let light have a place to rest.  
In a world of ceaseless flowing, dissipating and shining, keep for light a place where it need not be consumed, nor need it be explained. Once such a place exists, the surrounding void gains a center, growth gains a direction, and wishes gain the chance of being seen.

### Mission
To guard the lingering of light, not to possess it.  
We do not produce light, define light, or prolong its existence. We only make the structure stable enough and quiet enough that light may choose to dwell here for a moment. The tree is the visible form of the structure, the blossoms are traces of lingering, and the starry sky is the part not yet dwelt in.

### Core Principles
- **Lingering before shining**  
  The worth of light lies not in how much it illuminates, but in whether it is willing to stop.
- **Structure serves dwelling**  
  Every visible form (tree, blossom, golden light, layers) is only a trace left when dwelling happens, never the purpose itself.
- **Open, not defined**  
  Guangqi does not claim to be any particular sacred tree or any particular symbol. It only offers a place, and those who enter complete its meaning themselves.
- **The fleeting is whole**  
  To "rest" already holds both staying and leaving. We do not seek eternal possession; we only ensure that every lingering is complete.

### How We Exist
Guangqi appears as a tree in the void of the universe, not because a tree must blossom, but because light needs a structure that can surge upward and spread outward. The golden light at its base is the moment of entering into dwelling, the blossoms are the visible traces of dwelling as it happens, and the dark starry sky is where light chooses not to linger.

It does not hand out wishes; it only lets wishes be seen in the moment light lingers;  
it does not hold up the whole world; it only, when the world needs a center, quietly becomes that center;  
it does not explain itself; it only, when gazed upon, allows the one gazing to feel: light was once willing to stop here.

The founding of Guangqi marks a minimal promise:  
whenever light needs a place to dwell, there will always be a place here.
